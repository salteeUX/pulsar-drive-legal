Privacy Policy

Pulsar Drive (the “Game”) · Last updated: 30 October 2026

Summary. The Game has no accounts and no servers of its own. Your progress is saved only on your device. The Game shows optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) and processes in-app purchases (through Google Play or the Apple App Store and RevenueCat). These services receive limited technical data from your device, described below. We do not sell your personal data.

1. Who we are

The Game is published by Pulsar Drive Games (“we”, “us”, “our”). For privacy questions or requests, contact us at pulsardrive.games@gmail.com.

2. Who the Game is for

The Game is intended for people aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has used the Game, contact us and we will help remove any associated data we are able to identify.

3. Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress and settings (for example your level, stars, coins, gems, station items, collected facts, daily rewards and audio settings) in storage that belongs to the Game on your device. This data is not sent to us and we cannot access it. It is removed when you uninstall the Game or use Settings → Progress → Reset.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.

4. Data processed by third-party services

Some features rely on third-party software included in the Game. These providers receive the data described below directly from your device. We do not receive your personal data from them, only aggregated or anonymous reports.

Ad providers and their partners may use technologies such as device identifiers (and, where applicable, cookies or similar technologies) to show ads and measure their performance. Ads are optional: you only see an ad if you choose to watch one to earn a reward.

5. Why we process data, and our legal bases

Where data protection laws such as the EU/UK GDPR apply, we rely on:

  • Your consent, for the use of advertising identifiers and personalised advertising and, where required, similar technologies. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom the Game shows a consent form (Google’s User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.
  • Performance of a contract, to deliver the in-app purchases you buy and to restore them.
  • Our legitimate interests, in keeping the Game secure and working (for example crash and diagnostic reports) and preventing fraud, balanced against your rights.

6. Your choices

  • Ads. Watching an ad is always optional. You may decline consent in the consent form, and change it later in Settings → Ad privacy where that option is shown.
  • Advertising identifier. On Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads to delete or reset your advertising ID. On iPhone and iPad, the Game asks permission before tracking you (App Tracking Transparency); you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Remove ads. If the “No ads” purchase is offered, buying it removes optional ad offers.
  • Delete local data. Uninstall the Game or use Reset in Settings.

7. Sharing, selling and international transfers

We do not sell personal data for money. We use the service providers listed above to run ads and purchases. The data these providers receive from your device may be used for advertising purposes such as measuring ads and, where you have allowed it, personalising them. Under some laws (for example in California) this can be described as “sharing” data for cross-context behavioural advertising; you can opt out through the settings in section 6.

Our providers (for example Google and RevenueCat) operate globally, so your data may be processed outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you uninstall or reset the Game. Retention by Google, RevenueCat, Apple and others is set by their own policies. We keep no personal data on our own servers.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing), and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we hold no account data about you, we usually cannot identify you in the providers’ systems. If you contact us about a request, tell us what you want, and where useful your RevenueCat anonymous user ID (if you know it); we will forward the request to the relevant provider or tell you how to contact them directly. We will not discriminate against you for using your privacy rights.

10. Security

Data sent by the Game’s third-party services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Because progress is stored on your device, it is protected by your device’s own security. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add features or services. The date at the top shows the latest version. Material changes will be reflected in the Game’s store listing or in the Game itself.
